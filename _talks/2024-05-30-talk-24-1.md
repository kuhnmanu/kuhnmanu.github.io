This plenary talk presents a computational phenotyping approach to effort-based decision making in unmedicated adults with remitted depression. Using computational modeling of behavior on an effort-expenditure task, the work characterizes residual alterations in reward and effort processing that persist after symptom remission and discusses their potential role as vulnerability markers for depressive relapse.
